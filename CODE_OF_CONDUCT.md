# Code of Conduct

TapeFly is a place for rigorous, technically direct collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code, rules and evidence rather than people.
- Assume good faith while asking for reproducible proof.
- Explain unfamiliar circuit and blockchain concepts to newcomers.
- Respect keys, wallet details, personal information and private reports.
- Disclose conflicts of interest affecting a design or score.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing private reports or personal information.
- Falsifying tests, chain evidence, authors, benchmarks or relationships.
- Deliberately presenting a known-wrong circuit as valid.
- Repeated disruption, spam or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer through the GitHub profile
linked from this organization. Reports are confidential. Responses may include a
warning, content removal, participation restriction or ban according to severity
and repetition.

This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and any
space where someone represents TapeFly.

The policy follows the principles of Contributor Covenant 2.1 without claiming
participation by that project.
